How to Combine Multiple Photos into One PDF

Published November 2, 2026 · Ubahin Team

Many online applications ask for your documents in a single PDF, but yours may still be separate photos. You do not need any extra app to put them together.

Preparation

Photograph your documents with enough light and keep them straight. Make sure the whole page fits in the frame and the text is readable. If the photos are too large, shrink them first with Compress Image so the final PDF does not get bloated.

Steps

  1. Open JPG to PDF.
  2. Select all the photos at once. JPG, PNG, WEBP, and HEIC formats are supported.
  3. Set the order with the up and down arrow buttons. The first page in the list becomes the first page of the PDF.
  4. Choose the page size: A4 for official documents, or "fit to image size" if you want no empty space.
  5. Press Create PDF and download the result.

If the PDF is too large

Use Compress PDF, or shrink the photos before combining them. The second approach usually gives sharper results.

Merging existing PDFs

If your documents are already several PDF files, use Merge PDF.

Privacy

Your KTP and ijazah are personal data. Because Ubahin processes files in your browser, your documents are not sent to our servers.
