How to Convert iPhone Videos (MOV) to MP4 Without Losing Quality

Published November 5, 2026 · Ubahin Team

Videos from an iPhone are usually saved as MOV files. Many portals, editing apps, and players on Android or Windows ask for MP4. The good news is that in many cases you do not need to re-encode the video.

What is the difference between MOV and MP4?

Both are containers that wrap video and sound. What is inside, for example H.264 video and AAC audio, is often identical. That is why switching the container from MOV to MP4 can be done without touching the picture or sound quality.

Steps

  1. Open MOV to MP4.
  2. Choose one or several MOV videos from your iPhone.
  3. Click Convert to MP4. It takes only a few seconds because the video is not recalculated.
  4. Download the result. The size and quality are the same as the original.

If the result will not play

The most common cause is the HEVC (H.265) codec, which the iPhone uses to save space. Older devices or players may not support it yet. There are two ways out: on the iPhone, change Settings, Camera, Formats to Most Compatible so future videos use H.264, or use Compress Video, which re-records the video into a more common format, with the trade-off that quality may drop slightly.

When is this tool not suitable?

Videos with the ProRes codec cannot be repackaged as MP4. In that case, use Compress Video or a desktop app.

Only need the sound?

If all you need is the audio from a video, use Video to MP3.

When you need MP4 and when MOV is enough

If the video will only be played on an iPhone, a Mac, or sent to other Apple users, MOV is fine. MP4 is safer if the video will be uploaded to a portal, opened on a Windows PC, imported into an editing app, or sent to a device you do not know.

Why it is so fast

Re-encoding a video means recalculating every frame, and that takes as long as the video itself. This tool does not do that. It only changes the part of the file header that names the container type, then copies the video content as is. That is why even videos of hundreds of megabytes finish in seconds and no quality is lost.

Frequently asked questions

Does the file size change? No. The result is the same size as the original file.

Can every MOV video be converted? Not all. ProRes videos are not supported, and videos with an unrecognized codec get a warning in the result.

Is my video uploaded? No. The file is processed in your browser.

What if it is too large for the portal? Once it is an MP4, shrink it with Compress Video or cut its duration with Trim Video.
