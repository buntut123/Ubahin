MP3, AAC, and WAV: The Differences and How to Extract Audio from a Video

Published November 2, 2026 · Ubahin Team

Sometimes all we need is the sound: a meeting recording, a lecture, or a family event video that you recorded yourself. Before converting, it helps to understand the differences between these three audio formats.

MP3

The best-known and most compatible format. It uses lossy compression. At 128 kbps, one minute of audio is about 1 MB. Good for music and general voice recordings.

AAC

The format used in many MP4 videos and phones. At the same bitrate, AAC generally sounds slightly better than MP3. If your video already contains AAC, the audio can be extracted as is without re-converting, so quality does not drop and the process is very fast.

WAV

Uncompressed audio. Full quality, but the files are large: stereo CD quality is about 10 MB per minute. Good as material for editing, less suited to long-term storage or sending.

Which one should you choose?

  • Want a small file that plays anywhere: MP3.
  • Video comes from a phone and you want the fastest result with no quality loss: AAC.
  • Will be edited further in an audio app: WAV.

How to extract audio from a video

  1. Open Video to MP3.
  2. Select a video from your device.
  3. Choose the format (MP3, AAC, or WAV). For MP3, choose the quality: 128 kbps is enough for speech.
  4. Press the process button and download the result.

Note

Only process videos that you own or have the right to use. Very long videos need a lot of memory, so for files over a few hundred MB, it is better to use a computer.
