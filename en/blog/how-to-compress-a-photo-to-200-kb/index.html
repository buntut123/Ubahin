How to Shrink a Photo to 200 KB for Online Forms

Published November 2, 2026 · Ubahin Team

School registration, scholarship, and job application forms often limit photo size, for example to a maximum of 200 KB. A photo straight from a phone camera is usually much larger, between 2 and 8 MB. Here is how to shrink it while keeping it looking good.

Two things determine photo size

File size is determined by the number of pixels (width and height) and the level of compression (quality). Lowering either one makes the file smaller. A photo that will only be viewed on screen does not need thousands of pixels, so reducing the resolution a little is barely noticeable.

Quick steps

  1. Open Compress Photo to 200 KB. For other limits, use Compress Image and choose a target size.
  2. Select a photo from your gallery or files.
  3. Press the compress button, then download the result.
  4. Open the result and make sure faces or text in the photo are still clear.

If your photo is in HEIC format

Photos from an iPhone are often in HEIC format, which forms do not accept. Convert it first with HEIC to JPG, or choose the HEIC file directly in the compression tool, since that format is supported too.

Tips for keeping good results

  • Photos with a plain background are easier to shrink than busy, detailed ones.
  • Use JPG for photos. PNG tends to be much larger.
  • Do not compress repeatedly. Always start from the original photo, because each compression lowers quality.
  • Check the organizer's official requirements. Some ask for specific pixel dimensions, not just a file size.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. The whole process runs in your browser, so your personal photos are not sent to a server.
