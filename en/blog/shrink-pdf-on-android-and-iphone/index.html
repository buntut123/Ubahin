How to Reduce PDF Size on Android and iPhone

Published November 5, 2026 · Ubahin Team

Often the PDF you need to upload is on your phone: a scan, a photo of a document, or a file you received in a chat. You do not need to install a special app. Just open your browser and use a compression tool that runs on your own device.

Steps

  1. Open Compress PDF in Chrome or Safari.
  2. Tap the upload area and choose the PDF from your storage or the Files app.
  3. Choose a compression level. Start with Medium.
  4. Tap Compress PDF and wait. PDFs with many pages take longer on a phone.
  5. Check the size of the result. If it is still too large, repeat with the Strong or Extreme level.

Choosing a compression level

  • Light: high quality, smallest savings.
  • Medium: balanced, enough for most files.
  • Strong and Extreme: much smaller size, but text can look blurrier.

Good to know

Compression works by re-rendering each page as an image. As a result, the text in the output can no longer be copied or searched. If your PDF contains plain text and is already small, you do not need to compress it.

Cut down the pages first

The fastest way to shrink a PDF is to remove pages you do not need with Delete PDF Pages, or to extract just some pages with Split PDF.

If the browser feels slow

A very large PDF can make your phone run out of memory. Close other tabs, reduce the pages first, or try on a computer.

Why PDFs from phones are often large

Scanner apps on phones usually save each page as a high-resolution photo. A single page can reach several megabytes, so a 10-page document easily goes over an upload limit. A PDF made from text, such as an exported document, is usually much smaller because it stores letters, not images.

Recommended order

  1. Remove pages you do not need, such as blank or duplicate pages.
  2. Try Medium compression and check the size of the result.
  3. If needed, go up one level and compare how sharp the text is.
  4. Stop at the lowest level that meets the limit, so the text stays clear.

Frequently asked questions

Is my file safe? The file is processed in your phone's browser and is not sent to our servers.

Why can't I search for words in the result? Because the pages are turned into images. This is a necessary trade-off to make the file smaller.

My PDF is password-protected. Unlock it first with your PDF viewer, then try again.

It is slow or my phone gets hot. That is normal for PDFs with many pages, because everything is calculated on your device. Keep the screen on until it finishes.
