JPG vs PNG vs WEBP: When to Use Which

Published November 2, 2026 · Ubahin Team

The three image formats you will see most often are JPG, PNG, and WEBP. Each stores images differently, and choosing the right one can save file size or preserve quality.

JPG

JPG uses lossy compression: some detail is discarded to keep the file small. It is well suited to photos. It does not support transparent backgrounds. Each time you re-save it with compression, quality can drop a little.

PNG

PNG uses lossless compression: no detail is discarded. It supports transparent backgrounds. It suits logos, icons, screenshots, and images with text. For photos, the file is usually much larger than a JPG.

WEBP

WEBP was developed by Google and can work in both lossy and lossless modes, and it supports transparency. At similar quality, its files are generally smaller than JPG or PNG. Almost all modern browsers support it, but some older apps and online forms do not accept it yet.

Quick guide

NeedBest format
Photos to send or upload to a formJPG
Logos, icons, images with crisp textPNG
Images for a website that should stay lightweightWEBP
Need a transparent backgroundPNG or WEBP

How to convert between formats

Use Image Converter to convert to JPG, PNG, or WEBP. There are also dedicated tools such as PNG to JPG and WEBP to PNG. Remember that converting a JPG to PNG does not bring back detail that has already been lost.
