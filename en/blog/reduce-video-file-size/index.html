How to Reduce Video File Size for Sending or Uploading

Published November 3, 2026 · Ubahin Team

Videos from a phone can be tens to hundreds of MB per minute. To send one over chat or upload it to a form, you often need to make it smaller.

What determines video size?

Two main things: resolution (for example 1080p or 480p) and bitrate, which is the amount of data per second. Lowering both makes the file smaller, at the cost of a less sharp picture.

Steps

  1. Cut out the parts you do not need with Trim Video. This is the easiest way to reduce the size.
  2. Open Compress Video, choose a video, then choose the output size: 720p for good quality, 480p for a balance, 360p or 240p when file size matters much more.
  3. Choose whether to keep the sound. Removing the sound shrinks the file a little more.
  4. Press process and keep the tab open until it finishes.

Why does it take as long as the video?

So the file never has to be sent to a server, the video is played in your browser and re-recorded at lower quality. Recording runs at playback speed, so a 2-minute video takes about 2 minutes. Keep the screen on and the tab active.

If you only need the sound

Use Video to MP3, whose result is much smaller than the video.
