How to Add a Watermark to Your Own Photos

Published November 3, 2026 · Ubahin Team

A watermark helps show that a photo is your work and lowers the risk of someone else claiming it. It works well for online shop product photos, photography, or designs you share on social media.

Steps

  1. Open Watermark Photo and choose one or many photos at once.
  2. Type your text, such as your shop or account name.
  3. Pick a position. The bottom-right corner stays out of the way, while the repeating mode is harder to remove.
  4. Adjust the size and opacity, then download the result.

Tips

  • Choose white for dark photos and black for bright photos.
  • An opacity of 50 to 70 percent is usually clear enough without covering the subject.
  • For especially valuable photos, use the repeating mode.
  • Keep the original photo without a watermark.

Important note

Only use a watermark on photos you own or have the right to edit. No watermark is completely impossible to remove, so think of it as an extra layer of protection, not a guarantee.
